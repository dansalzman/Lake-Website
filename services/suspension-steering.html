How We Work

Straight Answers, Quality Work

Suspension and steering components wear gradually, so most drivers get used to the change without noticing it. A thorough inspection on a lift shows exactly which parts are loose, leaking or worn, and which are fine. We walk you through it and prioritize what matters for safety first.

We work on modern independent suspensions, light-truck and van front ends, and the leaf springs, kingpins and steering boxes found on older vehicles.

What’s Included

Suspension & Steering

When to Bring It In

Signs It’s Time

  • Clunks or knocks over bumps
  • The car wanders or pulls on the freeway
  • Uneven or rapid tire wear
  • Excessive bouncing or nose-dive when braking
  • Loose or heavy-feeling steering
