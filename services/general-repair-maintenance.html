How We Work

Straight Answers, Quality Work

Every vehicle has a maintenance schedule, and every driver has a real-world schedule that doesn’t always line up with it. We look at both. When your car comes in for an oil change, we also take a look at the things that tend to get missed — fluid condition, belts and hoses, tire wear, and anything leaking that shouldn’t be.

If something needs attention, we tell you what it is, show you why, and let you decide. If it can wait, we tell you that too. That’s the whole approach: straight answers, no upsell.

When to Bring It In

Signs It’s Time

  • The oil-change reminder or maintenance light is on
  • You’re near a 30k, 60k or 90k-mile interval
  • You notice drips or spots under the car
  • A belt squeals on start-up or in cold weather
  • You’re about to take a long trip
