How We Work

Straight Answers, Quality Work

Engine problems rarely fix themselves, and the longer a small leak or overheating issue goes, the more it costs. We diagnose the cause first, then lay out your options honestly: repair the problem, rebuild the engine, or — when it truly makes more sense — replace it.

We work on modern fuel-injected engines and on classic carbureted engines with points ignition, mechanical fuel pumps and all the details that come with them.

What’s Included

Engine Repair

Gaskets & Seals

Cooling Systems

Radiators, water pumps, thermostats and hoses for engines that run hot.

Timing Components

Timing belts, chains, tensioners and guides replaced on schedule or after failure.

Ignition & Fuel

Coils, plugs and injectors — plus points, distributors and carburetors on classics.

Internal Repairs

Compression and leak-down testing to pinpoint valve, ring and bearing problems.

Complete Rebuilds

Full engine rebuilds handled with precision, from teardown to break-in.

When to Bring It In

Signs It’s Time

  • The engine is overheating or losing coolant
  • Oil leaks or burning-oil smell
  • Knocking, ticking or other new engine noises
  • Blue or white smoke from the exhaust
  • Loss of power or low compression
