How We Work

Straight Answers, Quality Work

Cars have changed tremendously, but good mechanical work hasn’t. We bring the same discipline we apply to modern diagnostics to the carburetors, points ignition and vintage wiring that many shops no longer understand — or want to touch.

Whether it’s a numbers-matching muscle car, a carbureted classic truck, or a project that’s been sitting in a garage for years, we start by assessing what you have and talking through what you want: a faithful restoration, a reliable driver, or a resto-mod with modern brakes and drivability. Then we give you a straight plan and steady, transparent progress.
