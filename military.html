Questions

Military & Veteran FAQ

Who qualifies for military and veteran pricing?

Active duty, reserve and Guard members, veterans, retirees and military families. Show a military ID or DD-214 at the counter and the discount on labor applies every visit.

I’m PCSing in two weeks. Can you still help?

Yes — let’s look at it this week. The PCS Road-Ready Inspection is a fixed price and you leave with a written report. During summer PCS season, book about two weeks ahead of your move date.

I’m deployed and my spouse handles the car. How does that work?

We text photos and the estimate to whoever is handling the car, explain it plainly, and nothing happens until someone approves it.

How far are you from MCAS Miramar?

About two miles. We’re at 7675 Formula Place, Suite D, just off Miramar Road in the Miramar business park. We open at 8 AM on weekdays, so you can drop off on the way in.
