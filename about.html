Our Story

Built on Honest Service
and Quality Results

SP Automotive is an independently owned auto repair shop serving the San Diego community — on a mission to earn lasting trust, one honest repair at a time.

Our Mission

All Makes, All Models,
All Eras

We work on all makes and models, from today’s daily drivers to the carbureted classics that many shops no longer want to touch.

Our mission is to provide our community with honest, dependable automotive repair and exceptional craftsmanship while treating every customer with fairness and respect. We strive to earn lasting trust through quality work and transparent recommendations.

Why We Started

Ten Years in the
US Coast Guard

Our founder started SP Automotive after serving 10 years in the US Coast Guard — and after a dozen overpriced repair bills that left him feeling robbed.

Rather than keep taking his own vehicles to shops he couldn’t trust, he decided to start a repair shop of his own — one that prioritizes honesty and quality craftsmanship above everything else.

Named After a Legacy

Stephen Paul Williams

SP Automotive is named after our founder’s late father, Stephen Paul Williams, who had him holding the flashlight in the garage before he was five years old.

That early exposure to the garage instilled a lifelong passion for automobiles and the craftsmanship behind them. SP Automotive carries his legacy forward — a lifelong dedication to service and community.

Then & Now

Modern Technology & Classic Automobiles

Cars have changed tremendously, but good mechanical work hasn’t.

Our shop is equipped to diagnose, maintain, and repair modern vehicles while retaining the knowledge and hands-on approach necessary to work on older automobiles. We service all makes and models, including carbureted applications, and particularly enjoy working with owners who want to keep classic and vintage vehicles on the road.

We strive to earn lasting trust through quality work, transparent recommendations, and service guided by faith, honesty, and respect for every customer who walks through our door.
